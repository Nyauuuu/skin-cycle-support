Tidal Skin / 潮汐肌语 Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Data We Handle

  • Skin diary entries, including optional photos and ratings
  • Menstrual cycle records entered manually or imported from Apple Health with your permission
  • Skincare product records and usage logs
  • Optional Apple Watch and HealthKit metrics when you grant access
  • Optional WeatherKit-derived weather metrics used for skincare suggestions

How Data Is Used

Tidal Skin / 潮汐肌语 uses your records to show calendar trends, skin-cycle insights, reminders, and personalized skincare suggestions. Skin photo analysis runs on device with Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks.

Face Data

When you choose to add a skin photo, the app may process the image on device to check whether the photo is suitable for skin diary analysis. This processing may include detecting the presence and approximate area of a face in the image. The app does not identify who you are, does not perform face recognition, does not create a face template, and does not use face data for authentication or advertising.

Face-related processing is used only to improve the quality of the skin diary analysis, such as helping determine whether a submitted photo is relevant for skin status logging. The app stores the photo only if you choose to save the skin diary entry. Derived skin scores and optional analysis summaries may be stored locally with the diary entry. Face detection results are not shared with third parties and are not uploaded to a Tidal Skin server.

Skin photos and related diary entries remain on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or delete them using system or in-app features. You can delete saved records from the app at any time.

Data Sharing

Tidal Skin / 潮汐肌语 does not sell your personal data. The app does not upload skin photos or health records to a Tidal Skin server. Data may leave your device only when you use system features such as export, share sheets, backups, App Store purchases, Apple Health, WeatherKit, or notifications.

Health Disclaimer

Tidal Skin / 潮汐肌语 is a skincare diary and wellness tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice. Please consult a qualified medical professional for skin conditions or health concerns.
